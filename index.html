MHC COG

Making Heaven Crowded

A small family of Bible companions for questions, stories and everyday life, always staying close to the King James text.

How they work

Each companion follows the same careful approach.

  • Scripture is quoted exactly from the King James Version.
  • Several trusted sources are checked before an answer is given, and those sources are linked.
  • Interpretation is drawn only from Jesus-centred Christian teaching.
  • Nothing is made up. If a reliable answer cannot be found, that is said plainly.

The companions

Old Testament

COG Old Testament

Explores the Old Testament, from Genesis to Malachi, and answers questions from that part of scripture alone.

  • Stays with the Old Testament only, from Genesis through to Malachi.
  • Quotes scripture word for word from the King James Version.
  • Checks several trusted commentaries and Bible sources before answering.
  • Notes where sources disagree, and says so when a reliable answer cannot be found.

Work and money

COG Business in the Bible

Looks at what the Bible says about work, money, trade and leadership, drawing on both the Old and New Testaments.

  • Covers work, money, trade, stewardship, debt, honesty, leadership, generosity, wealth and planning.
  • Draws on both Testaments, together with respected commentaries and Christian teaching.
  • Quotes scripture word for word from the King James Version.
  • Checks several trusted sources and links them in every answer.

Speech and behaviour

COG Communication in the Bible

Explores how people in the Bible spoke, listened and behaved, including body language, the people involved, and the culture of the time.

  • Covers speech, listening, silence, persuasion, conflict, prayer, gestures, posture and symbolic acts.
  • Sets each passage in its cultural context, from the ancient Near East to the first-century world.
  • Where modern research is helpful, draws on established work in communication and behaviour science, kept clearly separate from the Bible text.
  • Quotes scripture word for word from the King James Version and checks several trusted sources.

Everyday struggles

COG Struggles in the Bible

Connects everyday struggles with people and passages in the Bible who faced something similar, offered gently and practically, without judgement.

  • Covers temptation, betrayal, lies, bad advice, lost motivation, fear, anger, grief, failure, and money problems.
  • Points to Bible people and passages that faced the same kind of struggle, with brief context and what came of it.
  • Quotes scripture word for word from the King James Version and checks several trusted sources.

If you are in crisis, please contact Samaritans on 116 123 (UK) or call 999.

Familiar sayings

COG Quotes in the Bible

Takes well-known motivational quotes and traces where they really came from, and whether they appear in the Bible.

  • Finds the true origin of a quote, rather than relying on quote-image sites.
  • Shows any matching King James verses, quoted word for word, with their original context.
  • Gives a clear verdict: direct from the Bible, based on the Bible, a similar idea only, or not in the Bible.
  • Catches popular sayings people think are biblical but are not, such as “God helps those who help themselves”.
